76. Your server is running very slowly. How will you troubleshoot it?

Expected approach:

CPU → Memory → Disk → Processes → Network → Logs

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

79. Disk usage is 100%. What will you do?

df -h
du -sh /*
du -sh /var/log/*

Then identify large files/directories and safely clean or rotate logs.

80. A process is consuming 100% CPU. What will you do?

top
ps aux --sort=-%cpu

Find the PID → investigate → gracefully terminate if appropriate.
